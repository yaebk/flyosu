What you are watching

Notes are drawn onto 8,452 simulated photoreceptors laid out like a real fly's eye. Activity then runs through the connectome's 19,367 neurons and 729,558 synapses in 2 ms steps. The brain itself is never trained. A linear readout of its 1,303 descending neurons, the cells that carry commands to the legs, decides when each key goes down and comes back up. The legs are an illustration: the leg motor system is not modelled, and each drawn leg rests on its key for exactly the time that key was down.

Each replay is a 60-second section of the map, shared by all of a song's difficulties. It is judged with osu!mania's timing windows for the map's OD. The score uses the classic osu!mania formula, and the letter grade follows its accuracy thresholds. There is no health bar, because the fly is never failed out.

Held-out songs were never used to fit or choose anything, so they are the fair test. The fly was fitted on half of the 6-second sections of the tuning songs' difficulties, so those replays overstate it. Over the full held-out maps this fly averages 87.6% accuracy. In a pre-registered test, 19 of 20 randomly rewired brains trained the same way did better.
